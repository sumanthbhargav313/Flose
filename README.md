# Flose

Flose is a close personal companion that turns a short wellbeing check-in into a
practical daily plan. Momo coordinates a Personal Trainer and Chef to balance
movement, meals, recovery, and existing commitments.

## What you can experience

- Explore the Flose home screen and companion roles.
- Share diet, location, sleep, stress, mood, movement, and schedule preferences.
- Choose workout time directly or let Momo estimate it from the day's schedule.
- Receive coordinated Momo, Trainer, and Chef guidance in one plan.

The public experience runs entirely in the visitor's browser. Check-in information
is not uploaded, stored, or sent to an AI provider.
